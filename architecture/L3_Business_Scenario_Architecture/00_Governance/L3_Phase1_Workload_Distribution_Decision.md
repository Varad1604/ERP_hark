# L3 Phase 1 Workload Distribution Decision

**Architecture Level:** L3 — Business Scenario Architecture  
**Phase:** Phase 1 — Scenario Discovery  
**Decision Status:** Locked Working Decision  
**Decision Scope:** Phase 1 discovery workload only  

---

## 1. Purpose

This document records the locked Phase 1 discovery workload distribution for the SutraERP L3 Business Scenario Architecture effort.

The purpose of this decision is to distribute discovery work based on practical team availability and expected discovery effort, without changing canonical architecture ownership.

---

## 3. Rationale

This distribution is based on Phase 1 discovery workload balancing.

The number of sections assigned to a team member is not the deciding factor. The distribution considers expected discovery effort, architectural complexity, cross-domain impact, and practical availability.

Dhruv carries architecture-sensitive and cross-domain-heavy areas, including Platform Foundation, Enterprise Resource Management, governance, and pilot framing.

Varad carries operationally heavy domains involving Campus Operations, Finance, and Institutional Services & Intelligence.

Pallav carries institution, academic delivery, and workforce-related domains, which have strong institutional and operational continuity.

Chetan is assigned one focused domain: Student Lifecycle.

Devang is assigned one focused domain: Examination.

---

## 4. Non-Impact Statement

This is a Phase 1 discovery workload distribution only.

It does not change L1/L2 canonical ownership.

It does not change domain ownership.

It does not lock final L3 scenario ownership.

It does not change canonical entity ownership, lifecycle ownership, business event ownership, dependency direction, or business rule ownership.

Final L3 scenario ownership will be confirmed during Phase 2 consolidation.

---

## 5. Repository Governance

All discovered candidates must continue to follow the locked Phase 1 inventory format:

| Candidate ID | Scenario Title | Trigger | Final Outcome | Primary Owner | Domains | Type | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Candidate ownership in Phase 1 remains provisional.

Final L3 scenario IDs must not be assigned during Phase 1.

Full L3 scenario documents must not be created during Phase 1.

No implementation-level content is allowed in Phase 1 discovery outputs.

---

## 6. Change Control

Any future change to this workload distribution must be recorded explicitly.

Future changes must continue to preserve the distinction between:

- Phase 1 discovery responsibility
- canonical L1/L2 ownership
- final L3 scenario ownership
