### Section D derivation notes

- `CAN-L3-D-008` and `CAN-L3-D-025` were renamed for traceability. D-008 now names cross-campus enrollment (`L2-D01` §38) alongside cross-institution enrollment (§39), both of which it already covered. D-025 now names its monitoring subject rather than reading as a generic governance-exception title.
- `CAN-L3-D-016` and `CAN-L3-D-017` list `G` as a participating domain because timetabling consumes physical resource context. This does **not** assert D03 ownership of infrastructure; FIND-D-01 and FIND-D-02 remain unresolved and are not silently reconciled here.
- `CAN-L3-D-020` ends at Attendance Finalization. The boundary was extended on 2026-08-14 to match the `L2-D04` §23 lifecycle and the canonical example named in `L3_Scenario_Type_and_Priority_Rules.md` §9; it is a settled boundary, not an open decision item. See the 2026-08-14 change note below.
- Type and Priority are provisional for Phase 1 and are confirmed during Phase 2 consolidation, per `L3_Scenario_Type_and_Priority_Rules.md` §15.
