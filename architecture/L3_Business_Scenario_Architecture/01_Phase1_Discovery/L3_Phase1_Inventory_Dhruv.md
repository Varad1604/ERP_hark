## 2. Phase 1 Discovery Rules

Each candidate should represent one complete institutional business outcome.

The inventory must not:

* redefine L1 or L2 ownership;
* introduce new aggregates, lifecycle states, business events, or business rules;
* create APIs, database structures, UI designs, implementation details, or technology decisions;
* create final L3 document numbers;
* treat small workflow actions as independent L3 scenarios;
* assume that the Phase 1 discoverer is the final scenario owner.

Cross-domain participation does not imply shared ownership.

The `X` discovery area shall be used only where the likely primary owner cannot be safely determined during Phase 1.
