## Register Interpretation

- `source owner` identifies the existing canonical business owner; it does not create an L6 service boundary.
- `operation class` is intentionally business-interaction level.
- `indeterminate` is a UX obligation to avoid false completion; no retry/idempotency mechanism is specified.
- `released integration contract not evidenced` means backend planning may derive required responsibilities but may not derive endpoint/payload details.
- W-C02 is the only selected deep family with the previously recorded dedicated L4→L5 traceability gate; that does not mean it is live integrated.
