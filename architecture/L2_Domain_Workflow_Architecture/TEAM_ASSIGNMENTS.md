### Architecture

- Design and maintain Sections A, G and H, including Infrastructure Management within Section G.

- Review every Level 2 architecture document.

- Merge approved documents into the main branch.
- Lock architecture documents after approval.
- Maintain architecture standards.
- Maintain naming consistency across the repository.
- Coordinate cross-team discussions.
- Own final Level 2 architecture.

# Ownership Rules

- Every Level 2 document has exactly one owner.
- Only the assigned owner may modify their architecture documents.
- Cross-section architectural changes require discussion with the respective owner.
- Every completed document must be reviewed and approved by the Lead Architect before merging.
- Locked documents may only be modified after approval from the Lead Architect.
