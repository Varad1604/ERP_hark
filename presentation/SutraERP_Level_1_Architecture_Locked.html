SutraERP Level 1 Architecture --- Locked Lifecycle

Status: Locked
Scope: Complete institute lifecycle at Level 1
Purpose: Define the main business stages, ownership boundaries, lifecycle rules, and cross-domain responsibilities before Level 2 workflows.

1. Core Architecture Rules

1.1 Tenant, Institute, and Academic Unit

flowchart TD
    A[SutraERP Platform] --> B[Tenant]
    B --> C[Institute]
    C --> D[College / School]
  • Tenant is the SaaS isolation boundary.
  • Institute is the institutional organisation using SutraERP.
  • College / School is an independent academic operating unit inside the institute.

Example:

flowchart TD
    T[Tenant: ABC Education Society] --> I[Institute: ABC Group of Institutions]
    I --> C1[ABC Engineering College]
    I --> C2[ABC Pharmacy College]
    I --> C3[ABC School]

Locked rule: Tenant and Institute are separate concepts.

1.2 College and School Data Separation

College and school operational data must always remain separately scoped.

The following records belong to a specific College or School:

  • Admission Cycle and Application
  • Student Academic Context
  • Academic Year and Academic Period
  • Programme / Class structure
  • Course Offering
  • Attendance
  • Examination academic context
  • Finance context

Example:

ABC Engineering College
└── Academic Year AY-ENG-2026

ABC School
└── Academic Year AY-SCHOOL-2026

Both may display 2026–27, but they are separate records.

Institute-wide shared data may include:

  • Person
  • Tenant
  • Institute
  • Platform Identity
  • Canonical Infrastructure
  • Approved shared master data

Locked rule: Shared Person does not mean shared College/School operational data.

College/School scope must later be enforced in authorization, contracts, queries, and database constraints. It must not be treated only as a UI filter.

1.3 Canonical Person and Student Context

One real person has one canonical Person identity inside the institute.

A Person may have multiple historical Student Contexts, but only one Student Context may be ACTIVE at a time within the Institute.

Example:

Person P001
↓
Student Persona

├── ABC School
│   └── Student Context: COMPLETED
│
└── ABC Engineering College
    └── Student Context: ACTIVE

Not allowed:

Person P001

ABC School
Student Context: ACTIVE

AND

ABC Engineering College
Student Context: ACTIVE

Before a new Student Context becomes active, the current active context must be closed through a valid lifecycle state such as:

COMPLETED
TRANSFERRED
WITHDRAWN
DISCONTINUED
TERMINATED

Locked rule: One Person may have many historical Student Contexts but a maximum of one active Student Context per Institute.

Dual or parallel enrollment is outside the current scope and must not be allowed accidentally.

1.4 Cross-Domain Ownership

A domain owns its data and lifecycle.

Other domains must not directly update its tables.

Use:

  • Synchronous contracts when an immediate authoritative answer or action is required.
  • Domain events for completed business facts and asynchronous reactions.

Example:

Examination
↓ asks Academics
"What is the student's finalized attendance?"

Not:

Examination
↓
Direct SQL against Attendance tables

3. L1-01 --- Tenant Provisioning

Purpose: Create an isolated SutraERP operating boundary.

Owner: Platform
Actor: SutraERP Platform Admin or automated provisioning
Team: Team 2

REQUESTED
↓
PROVISIONING
↓
ACTIVE

Possible failure state:

PROVISIONING_FAILED

Main responsibilities:

  • Create Tenant identity.
  • Initialize platform configuration.
  • Initialize Module Registry.
  • Initialize audit and identity infrastructure.
  • Prevent duplicate Tenant creation.

Locked rule: Tenant provisioning is a platform operation. Registrar or Principal does not provision a Tenant.

4. L1-02 --- Institute Creation

Purpose: Create the institutional identity inside an active Tenant.

Owner: Institution Domain
Actors: Platform Admin / Institute Admin
Team: Team 2

DRAFT
↓
SETUP_PENDING
↓
ACTIVE

Main responsibilities:

  • Create Institute.
  • Link Institute to Tenant.
  • Configure core Institute profile.
  • Audit major Institute changes.

Locked rule: Tenant and Institute must not use the same business entity.

5. L1-03 --- College / School and Institutional Structure Setup

Purpose: Define the academic operating units, organisation structure, and physical operating locations.

Owner: Organisation Domain
Actors: Institute Admin / Registrar
Team: Team 2

Example:

ABC Group of Institutions
├── ABC Engineering College
│   ├── Computer Engineering Department
│   └── Mechanical Engineering Department
│
└── ABC School
    ├── Primary Section
    └── Secondary Section

Campuses may include:

Main Campus
City Campus
Research Campus

Important distinction:

Organisation Unit ≠ Campus
  • Organisation Unit represents institutional responsibility or structure.
  • Campus represents a physical operating location.

Locked rule: Organisation structure and physical location remain separate concepts and are connected using canonical IDs.

6. L1-04 --- Academic Structure Setup

Purpose: Define how education is organised inside each College or School.

Owner: Academic Structure Domain
Actors: Registrar / Academic Admin

↓
Section
↓
Subject

Programme
↓
Programme Version
↓
Batch / Cohort
↓

↓

B.Tech Computer Engineering

Locked rule: Do not directly overwrite an active curriculum. Create a new Programme Version when the curriculum changes.

Locked rule: School and College academic models coexist. One must not be forced into the other.

7. L1-05 --- Academic Year and Academic Period Setup

Purpose: Create, approve, and open the academic cycle for a College or School.

Owner: Academic Structure / Academic Calendar
Actor: Registrar
Default Approver: Principal
Team: Team 2

7.1 Academic Year

The main academic cycle.

Example:

2026–27

7.2 Academic Period

A part of an Academic Year.

College example:

Academic Year 2026–27
├── Semester 1
└── Semester 2

School example:

Academic Year 2026–27
├── Term 1
├── Term 2
└── Term 3

Final hierarchy:

College / School
↓
Academic Year
↓
Academic Period

Locked rule: AcademicYear and AcademicPeriod are separate canonical entities.

Locked rule: Academic Year is College/School scoped.

7.3 Academic Year State

DRAFT
↓
PENDING_APPROVAL
↓
APPROVED
↓
OPEN
↓
CLOSING
↓
CLOSED

Approval alternatives:

PENDING_APPROVAL
├── APPROVED
├── RETURNED
└── REJECTED

Default workflow:

Registrar creates
↓
Registrar submits
↓
Principal approves / returns / rejects

The approval workflow must later be configurable through the Workflow Engine.

Important distinction:

APPROVED ≠ OPEN

APPROVED means the Academic Year is officially accepted.

OPEN means academic operations may start when their readiness requirements are satisfied.

Locked rule: APPROVED and OPEN remain separate states.

8. L1-06 --- Operational Readiness Check

Purpose: Check whether a specific business operation can start.

Readiness is evaluated for the requested capability.

Example:

Start Teaching

Academic Year OPEN       YES
Academic Period OPEN     YES
Course Offering Active   YES
Faculty Assigned         NO

Result:
NOT READY

Do not use one global flag:

institute_ready = true

Do not treat a stored readiness flag as authoritative.

Final model:

Operation Requested
↓
Readiness Rules Evaluated
↓
READY / NOT READY

Examples of capabilities:

Admission
Teaching
Attendance
Examination

Locked rule: Readiness is primarily evaluated dynamically. Caching may be added later but is not authoritative.

Engineering College
├── 2026 Engineering Admission
└── 2026 Direct Second Year Admission

School
├── 2026 Primary Admission
└── 2026 Secondary Admission

An Admission Cycle is created only when admissions are required.

Basic flow:

↓
Applicant Applies
↓
Person Checked / Created
↓
Application Reviewed
↓
Documents Checked
↓
Admission Decision
├── APPROVED
├── REJECTED
└── WAITLISTED
↓
Admission Completed

Important distinction:

ADMISSION APPROVED
≠
ACTIVE STUDENT

Person handling:

Applicant details received
↓

Automatic Person matching may suggest a match. Sensitive Person merges require controlled confirmation.

Locked rule: Admission Cycle and admission data are College/School scoped.

Locked rule: Admission Cycle is created only when required.

Locked rule: Admission approval does not automatically activate the Student.

10. L1-08 --- Student Activation and Academic Registration

Purpose: Convert an accepted applicant into an active Student and place the Student in the correct academic structure.

Owner: Student Academic Lifecycle
Actors: Student Administration / Academic Admin / System
Team: Team 2

10.1 Student Activation

Admission Completed
↓
Required Documents Checked
↓
Required Financial Condition Checked
↓
Student Context Created
↓
PENDING_ACTIVATION
↓
ACTIVE

Finance belongs to Team 1.

Student Domain must ask Finance through an approved contract.

Example:

Student Domain
↓ asks Finance
"Is the required admission financial condition satisfied?"
↓
YES / NO

Student Domain must not directly read or update Finance tables.

10.2 Active Student Context Rule

Before activating a Student Context:

Check Person
↓
Find ACTIVE Student Context in Institute
├── Found → Reject new activation
└── Not found → Continue activation

A new Student Context may become active only after the previous active context is validly closed.

10.3 Academic Registration

College example:

↓

↓

↓
Academic Period
↓
Course Enrollment

School example:

↓

↓
Section
↓

Important ownership boundary:

Student Academic Lifecycle
→ Student lifecycle and academic placement

Academics
→ Course definitions, offerings, teaching, timetable, attendance

Locked rule: Student progression and placement remain historical. They must not be reduced to one current semester/class field.

12.1 Eligibility

Example:

GRADE
CREDITS

Examination does not decide student promotion.

Locked rule: Team 1 Examination owns the official result.

13. L1-11 --- Academic Progression

Purpose: Decide what happens to the Student after the official result is published.

Owner: Student Academic Lifecycle
Team: Team 2

Basic flow:

Official Result Published
↓
Student Academic Context Loaded
↓
Progression Policy Applied
↓
Progression Decision
↓
Decision Applied
↓
Student Academic History Updated

Possible decisions:

PROMOTE
CONTINUE_WITH_BACKLOG
REPEAT_PERIOD
DETAIN
PROGRAMME_COMPLETE
MANUAL_REVIEW

Example:

Semester 1 Result:
5 Passed
1 Failed

Policy:
Maximum 2 failed courses allowed

Decision:
CONTINUE_WITH_BACKLOG

Progression policy may be scoped by:

Institute
↓
College / School
↓
Programme
↓
Programme Version

Local policy may override inherited policy where allowed.

13.1 Historical Academic Progression

Do not rely only on:

student.current_semester = 4

Authoritative history should remain:

Semester 1   COMPLETED
Semester 2   COMPLETED
Semester 3   COMPLETED
Semester 4   ACTIVE

A current position may exist as a read convenience.

Locked rule: Historical academic progression is authoritative.

Locked rule: Team 2 Student Academic Lifecycle owns progression.

Owner: Student Lifecycle
Actors: Registrar / Student Administration

↓
Required Checks
↓

↓

WITHDRAWN
TRANSFERRED

TERMINATED

14.1 Graduation

PROGRAMME_COMPLETE
↓
Graduation Requirements Checked
↓
Graduation Approved
↓
Student Context Closed as GRADUATED
↓
Alumni Persona Added

The Student Persona and academic history remain part of the Person's history.

14.2 Clearance

Clearance is based on policy and applicability.

Policy Requires Clearance
+
Student Used Applicable Capability
↓
Clearance Required

Example:

Library     REQUIRED
Finance     REQUIRED
Hostel      NOT APPLICABLE
Transport   NOT APPLICABLE

An enabled module does not automatically create a clearance requirement for every Student.

Locked rule: Exit type is explicit.

Locked rule: Clearance is policy and applicability based.

15. L1-13 --- Academic Year / Period Closure

Purpose: Officially close an Academic Year or Academic Period after required academic work is complete.

Owner: Academic Structure / Academic Calendar
Actor: Registrar
Default Approver: Principal
Team: Team 2

Basic flow:

OPEN
↓
Closure Requested
↓
Required Checks
↓
CLOSING
↓
Approval
↓
CLOSED

Typical closure checks:

Teaching completed?
Attendance finalized?
Required examination processing completed?
Progression processing completed?

Optional modules must not block closure unless policy explicitly requires them.

15.1 Closed Period Corrections

Do not reopen the complete Academic Year for a normal correction.

Use:

CLOSED Period
↓
Correction Request
↓
Approval
↓
Specific Record Corrected
↓
Audit Recorded

The Academic Year or Academic Period remains CLOSED.

Locked rule: Closed periods use controlled corrections instead of normal reopening.
