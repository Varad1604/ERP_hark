## 4. AUD-PAL2-010 — Research Result and Options

The audit recorded `CAN-L3-B-022` (Institutional Policy Revision) as having thin support because `L2-B01` §2 "This document OWNS" does not list policy. Research against higher-authority sources reverses that assessment.

**Locked L1 evidence** — `architecture/L1_Business_Domain_Architecture/SutraERP_L1_Business_Domain_Architecture_Locked.md`:

- Business Rules for the Institution domain: **"Institute owns institutional policies."**
- Canonical Entities of the Institution domain: **`Institute`, `Institute Policy`, `Institute Profile`** — three distinct canonical entities.
- Primary Output of the Institution capability: `Institute`, `Institute Profile`, `Institutional Policies`.

**Corroborating boundary evidence:**

- `L2-A00_Platform_Foundation_Architecture.md`: "It does not own institutional policies."
- `L2-A04_Authorization_and_Policy_Workflows.md` §2: owns Authorization Architecture, Policy Engine, Authorization Engine, Separation of Duties. This is technical access-control policy, not institutional business policy. A04 makes no claim on institutional policy.

**Conclusion.** Under the authority order, Locked L1 (rank 4) outranks Original L2 Source Documents (rank 8). L1 establishes both that the Institution domain owns institutional policies and that `Institute Policy` is a canonical entity distinct from `Institute Profile`. `CAN-L3-B-022` is therefore correct and correctly owned, and it must not be merged into `CAN-L3-B-021`, because L1 defines the two subjects as separate canonical entities.

**The defect is in `L2-B01`, not in the inventory.** L2-B01 fails to carry an explicit locked-L1 ownership statement down into its own scope section.

**Resolution — Option A applied 2026-08-14 with owner approval.** `L2-B01` §2 now lists `Institution Policy` and `Institution Policy Lifecycle`, and a new §2.1 records the clarification with a decision stamp. No new ownership was created; the section carries down an existing locked L1 statement and explicitly separates institutional business policy from `L2-A04` authorization policy. `CAN-L3-B-021` and `CAN-L3-B-022` both remain, and were not merged.

Options B (record the gap without editing L2) and C (merge B-022 into B-021) were not taken. C would have contradicted locked L1.
