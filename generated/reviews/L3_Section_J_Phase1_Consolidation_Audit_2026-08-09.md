## Ownership and boundary findings

- **J01 vs J02/J03/J04:** J01 owns method selection and the approved sourcing outcome. J02 owns supplier identity and eligibility; J03 owns approved demand/requisition; J04 owns the Purchase Order. J01 candidates therefore stop before a Purchase Order is issued.
- **J03 vs J01/J04:** J03 ends with a released or otherwise governed Purchase Requisition, including the emergency-authority path. J01 owns consolidation of approved procurement demand into sourcing and the approved sourcing outcome. Purchase Order creation, amendment, cancellation, and closure remain in J04.
- **J04 vs J05:** J04 owns supplier commitment. Physical delivery inspection, acceptance, rejection, and receipt correction remain in J05.
- **J05 vs J06:** J05 establishes accepted receipt. J06 owns canonical inventory recognition, availability, ledger consequence, adjustment, and valuation reference.
- **J06 vs J07:** J06 owns the item, warehouse/location structure, stock position, ledger, and adjustment consequences. J07 owns operational reservation, issue, return, transfer, distribution, consumption, and custody movement outcomes.
- **J08 vs J10:** J08 owns asset registration through retirement and the retirement recommendation. J10 owns irreversible disposal execution and write-off, with the resulting asset reconciliation handed back to J08.
- **J09 vs J06/J10:** J09 owns physical verification, investigation, determination, and reconciled closure. It may authorise or refer a stock adjustment but does not own day-to-day stock operations; confirmed loss/damage requiring disposal or write-off terminates at normalized `CAN-L3-J-109`, a J10 referral.
- **J10 vs J06/J08:** J10 owns disposal, scrap, destruction, donation, transfer-out, sale/auction, recycling/return, loss write-off, emergency disposal, and execution exception closure. It then reconciles inventory and asset consequences through J06 and J08.
